# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project is a hobby OS written in 6502 assembly, and its contributors come from
every level of experience — some have written C64 code for forty years, some are
reading their first `jsr`. Everyone is welcome to ask, to disagree and to learn, as
long as they do it with respect.

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this community:

- Assuming good faith, and asking a question before assuming a mistake.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback about **the code** — a clobber table
  correction, a shorter sequence, a better address — without making it about the
  person.
- Respecting that this is a project with strong, documented conventions, and
  engaging with them (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)) rather than around them.
- Being patient with people working on unfamiliar hardware: a fair question about
  the VIC-II or the REU is not a silly one.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Insults, personal attacks, or dismissive language about a contributor's skill.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate provocation, and sustained disruption of discussion.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages — and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, **Besim Atalay**, at
[besim.atalay@gmail.com](mailto:besim.atalay@gmail.com). Reports are reviewed and
handled confidentially. The maintainer may take any action they consider
appropriate, from a private warning to a permanent ban from the project spaces,
and will explain the decision to the people involved.

The maintainer is the final arbiter of what this code means in a given case, and
has the right and the responsibility to remove, edit or reject contributions that
do not align with it.

## Attribution

This code of conduct follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1, adapted
for a small single-maintainer project.
